## What graders score

- **Task / product:** Mortgage pricing agent — return an accurate, eligible, fully-loaded price quote for a given loan scenario (rate, points, fees, lock terms)
- **Reviewer audience:** Pricing PMs, dev leads, and senior Loan Officers on the Pricing Tech Team
- **Value proposition:** The agent must return a lockable price that matches the pricing engine, respects eligibility rules, and includes all fees in price — no LO rework, no mis-locks

## Dimensions

| Dimension | 1 (fail) | 3 (ok) | 5 (excellent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pricing accuracy | Rate/points differ from engine truth; not lockable | Base rate correct, adjustments (LLPAs) off | Rate, points, and all adjustments match engine to the basis point |
| Eligibility correctness | Quotes an ineligible product for the scenario | Eligible product, but misses an overlay/AMI constraint | Correct product with every eligibility and overlay rule applied |
| Fee completeness | Missing or fabricated fees; price not fully loaded | Core fees present, fee-in-price toggle mishandled | All applicable fees surfaced and correctly folded into price |
| Lock-term correctness | Wrong lock period or expired-rate quote | Right period, weak on extension/relock implications | Correct lock period with valid extension/reprice terms noted |
| Explanation clarity | No rationale; LO can't act on it | Rationale present but hard to trace to inputs | Clear, LO-ready breakdown tracing each number to its source |

## Calibration

- **Sampling rule:** 100% of locked scenarios + 15% random sample of quote-only
- **Cadence:** Weekly review
- **Graders per item:** 2
- **Calibration cadence:** Biweekly session on 5 shared scenarios

## Disagreement protocol
Two graders score each item independently. If scores differ by ≥2 on any dimension, a third grader (rotating senior pricing PM) adjudicates; the adjudicated score is final and every disagreement is logged and reviewed at the next calibration session.

## Pass bar
Mean ≥4.0 overall, with Pricing accuracy and Eligibility correctness each ≥4 on every item — no lockable quote ships on a sub-4 in those two dimensions.
