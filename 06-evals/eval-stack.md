## What "good" means

Quote-to-lock ≥ 60%; wrong-price flags < 2% of searches

LO thumbs-down on a returned quote, "price looks wrong" flag, manual OB re-run after DESK result. Passive — quote-to-lock conversion rate, time-on-results-page, abandonment before lock, re-search rate within 5 min.

## Golden set

150 frozen scenarios with known-good OB outputs — one per rule type (eligibility, markup override, fee cap, product availability) × loan-type matrix; versioned in repo, refreshed quarterly

## Release gate

**Hard gates (auto-block):**

Any price mismatch vs. OB on golden set; schema/null-rate failure; eligibility regression on Bond-HFA or AMI-gated products (HomeReady/Home Possible)

**Soft gates (PM sign-off):**

Eligibility match between 96–98%; new loan-type coverage gap; margin-override edge cases flagged but not regressed

**User-feedback layer (online):** cadence Streaming, reviewed weekly; owner PM (Pricing Tech), triages flags into eligibility vs. margin vs. fee buckets.
